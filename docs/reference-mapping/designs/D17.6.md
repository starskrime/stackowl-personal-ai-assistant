# Authz & Tenancy — the Honest Statement, Re-Measured Axis by Axis

**Item.** D17.6 · Authz & tenancy — `AHEAD`
**Last verified.** 2026-09-05, commit `06cc159f`

## Why this exists

`AHEAD`, Ask: *"Keep. This is enterprise-grade and Hermes has nothing comparable."* A keep
confirmation — and the entry singles out one property worth checking: `BoundsSpec` ships
"with an honest statement of which are actually enforced".

**A self-describing comment is a claim, not a measurement.** This item verified it.

## The statement, and why it is the best thing in the subsystem

`authz/enforcement.py` keeps `ENFORCED_AXES = frozenset({"tools"})` — one of five axes — and
does something stronger than documenting the gap:

> A *task envelope* that diverges on an axis no seam enforces would manufacture false
> confidence (e.g. `network: none` that does not block the network). So a task-scoped
> divergence on an unenforced axis is REFUSED at construction — fail closed — whether the
> task value is tighter OR looser.

A constraint that looks enforced and is not is the defect this whole programme hunts. This
module refuses to let one exist. **Tightening is refused too**, which is the part that shows
the reasoning is right: no seam can honour a narrowing any more than a widening.

The table is honest. Nothing has quietly become enforced.

## The one place two true statements read as a contradiction

`bounds.py` said `data_owner_id` is "enforced by tenancy `OwnedRepository` (already)".
`enforcement.py` excludes it from `ENFORCED_AXES`, so a task diverging on it is refused.

Both are correct, and neither said which sense it meant:

- `OwnedRepository.__init__(db, owner_id)` constrains **every query** to its owner — the axis
  is enforced as a property of the repository layer.
- **Nothing plumbs a task-scoped `data_owner_id`** — zero references outside `authz/`. So a
  per-task divergence cannot be honoured, and refusing it is right.

A reader meeting them apart concludes one is stale. They now cross-reference each other, with
the distinction stated: *enforced as a property of every query, unenforceable as a per-task
divergence.* `network`'s entry was corrected the same way — "MODELLED ONLY", pointing at the
SSRF guard that does the real work (D17.4) and at DEBT-118 for its residual.

## What this item added

`tests/tenancy/test_the_closed_axis_set_stays_closed_in_both_places.py`, a tripwire on the
real drift risk. `BoundsSpec` calls the enumeration "intentionally CLOSED", and
`enforcement.py` keeps a **second** enumeration, `_AXIS_UNSET`, which it walks to decide what
to refuse.

**An axis absent from that table is never walked** — not returned by
`unenforced_axis_change`, not refused by `assert_task_narrowing_enforceable`. A sixth axis
added to the model alone would be a task constraint that no seam enforces and no check
rejects: the exact false confidence this module exists to prevent, arriving through the one
door it does not watch.

Two enumerations of one closed set is `CLAUDE.md` shape 3. The guard derives one from
`BoundsSpec.model_fields` rather than from a list, in both directions.
