# The Learning Section of the Morning Brief

> **Status:** live
> **Map item:** D09.6 · Learning made visible
> **Source:** `src/stackowl/brief/assemblers.py::LearningAssembler` (~93 lines),
> wired into `src/stackowl/scheduler/handlers/morning_brief.py`
> **Config:** none user-facing — one module constant, `_LEARNING_WINDOW_HOURS = 24`
> **Last verified:** 2026-09-04, against commit `a3181106`

## Why this exists

The platform learns constantly and none of it was visible. Measured when the section
shipped: **5,747 lessons and 586 learning artifacts all-time**, with none of it ever
reaching the person the learning is for. An agent that improves invisibly is
indistinguishable from one that does not improve, and the operator has no way to tell
whether a week of reflections was worth anything.

The map's Ask — "is user-visible learning part of the product, or internal machinery?"
— was already answered by a recorded decision rather than by this item. `SystemSpendAssembler`
exists because Bakir said *"that will give visibility to user what is happening in system"*.
That is the same question, settled for spend; learning gets the same treatment on the
same surface.

## Model

One `BriefSectionAssembler` among several. It answers two questions over a fixed
24-hour window:

* **What was learned** — `lessons` grouped by `source_type` (`reflection`, `skill`,
  `tool_heuristic`), so the brief can say *what kind* of learning happened rather than
  only how much.
* **What was produced** — `learning_artifacts` counted by `artifact_type`.

The two are read independently on purpose (see Failure modes).

## Lifecycle

The morning-brief job runs, the assembler is invoked with a `BriefContext`, it issues
its two queries and returns a `BriefSection`. The section is rendered with the others
and delivered on the brief's normal path. It holds no state between runs; the 24-hour
window is computed fresh each time, so a missed brief leaves no gap to reconcile.

## Invariants

1. **The window is a window, not a cursor.** Always the last 24 hours from now — never
   "since last run" — so a skipped or duplicated brief cannot double-count or lose a day.
2. **A failure omits the section; it never fails the brief.** The brief carries several
   sections and one bad query must not cost the others.
3. **Kinds are reported, not just totals.** "22 lessons" says less than "22 lessons:
   reflection, tool_heuristic", and the kinds are what tell the operator which learning
   loop is actually running.

## Configuration

`_LEARNING_WINDOW_HOURS = 24`, a module constant. Not user-facing: the brief is daily,
so the window matches its cadence by construction, and a configurable value would be a
second place for those two numbers to disagree.

## Observability

`[brief] learning: assembled` at **INFO**, carrying `lessons`, `kinds` and
`window_hours`. INFO deliberately — production runs at INFO, and this line is the only
evidence that the section was built at all; at DEBUG a silent brief would be
indistinguishable from a brief with nothing to say.

Both failure paths log too: `could not read lessons` at ERROR (the section is omitted),
`could not read learning_artifacts` at WARNING (the section still reports lessons).

## Failure modes

| What fails | What happens |
|---|---|
| The `lessons` query raises | ERROR logged, section omitted, rest of the brief unaffected |
| The `learning_artifacts` query raises | WARNING logged, section still reports lessons — a partial answer beats none |
| Nothing was learned in 24h | The section reports honestly rather than being suppressed; a quiet day is information |
